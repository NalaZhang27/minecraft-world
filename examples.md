# Reconstruction patterns

Use these as translation patterns, not fixed compositions.

## Portrait or animal

Keep the character or creature, its pose, defining colors, and one location cue. Place it at believable player/world scale on reconstructed terrain. Convert the surrounding location into matching block vegetation, architecture, weather, and light; remove crowds and incidental objects.

## Object or still life

Select the defining object and at most two supporting cues. Reinterpret them as world-scale landmarks, structures, resources, or terrain features rather than freestanding cube sculptures. Replace the tabletop or studio background with navigable ground, horizon, sky, and atmospheric depth.

## Architecture or travel

Keep the landmark, approach or waterway, and terrain profile. Rebuild broad facade rhythms and rooflines from structural blocks, then connect them to paths, vegetation, neighboring terrain, and a distant hazy world.

## Landscape

Keep the dominant landform, water or route, and foreground anchor. Build stepped terrain in large layers. Let vegetation and texture density diminish across the midground until distant silhouettes dissolve into haze.

## Prompt assembly

State, in order: selected source cues; their world-native reinterpretation; terrain and environmental integration; block surface language; camera and lighting; foreground-to-horizon depth behavior; exclusions.

> Reimagine the source as a believable cinematic screenshot from one expansive Minecraft-like world. Preserve only [subjects, colors, relationships, mood]. Recast [subjects] as [world-native forms] integrated with [terrain, vegetation, architecture, sky]. Use large structural blocks, stepped silhouettes, flat square faces, matte low-resolution surface textures, directional game light, and ambient occlusion. Use a moderate/wide environmental camera. Keep the foreground sharp; progressively reduce detail, texture definition, contrast, and saturation through the midground into strong horizon haze. Avoid cubelet sculpture, LEGO, miniatures, tabletops, photographic backgrounds, shallow depth of field, bokeh, and smooth or glossy 3D.

If the result resembles a cubed photograph, redesign the subject as a world-native form and enlarge the structural scale. If it resembles a product shot, widen the camera, add navigable terrain and horizon depth, and remove lens blur.
